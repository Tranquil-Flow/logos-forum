Reading polish, 2026-10-07 (owner picked all four from a list).

- Scroll: the thread is a ListModel updated in place, so a refresh keeps the
  reader's position; it follows the newest post only when the reader was at
  the bottom, switched topic or posted. Otherwise "New posts below" appears.
  The jump to the end repeats briefly while rows settle (rows differ in
  height) and stops as soon as the reader scrolls.
- Times: "just now" (under 2 min; signed times have minute precision),
  "N min ago", else HH:mm, under day separators (Today, Yesterday,
  "Thursday 1 October"); the signed date is always shown under the time
  (owner: hover delay too long, show it always).
- Click to copy: key ids and snapshot CIDs, with a "Copied ..." note.
- Keyboard: Ctrl/Cmd+F search (Cmd+K did not fire inside Basecamp, owner
  test), Ctrl/Cmd+N new topic, Esc closes the open panel or clears search.
